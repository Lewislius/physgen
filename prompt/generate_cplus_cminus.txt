DIRECT STAGE PAIRS IN P0-STAGE-DIRECT-JSON-R2

Each stages[].positive sentence in plan/planimg JSON is the stage c+ and is encoded
verbatim. There is no authored stage c-. The single-space cfg_negative is reused as
the empty comparison so Wan does not activate its built-in negative prompt.

No separate c+/c- file is consumed. Existing PXX-cplus-cminus.json files remain a
short pointer to stages[].positive and are marked used_for_generation=false. Never
append a template, violation sentence, forbidden noun list, or inferred text.
